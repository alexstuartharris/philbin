## What
Adds **UTM Buddy**: a GitHub Pages-friendly, client-side coordinate converter that works on pasted/uploaded CSV.

- Lat/Lon → UTM (WGS84)
- UTM → Lat/Lon (WGS84)
- Preserves original columns and adds new ones
- Row-level error reporting via a `_utmBuddyError` column

## Where
- App: `apps/utm-buddy/`
- Manifest updated via `node scripts/generate-manifest.mjs`

## How to test
1. Open `apps/utm-buddy/index.html` locally (or after merge: https://alexstuartharris.github.io/philbin/apps/utm-buddy/)
2. Paste:
   ```csv
   site,lat,lon
   A,49.702,-123.155
   B,49.705,-123.141
   ```
3. Choose **Lat/Lon → UTM**, zone **10**, hemisphere **N**, click **Parse CSV**, then **Convert**
4. Confirm `easting_m` and `northing_m` columns appear and **Download CSV** works

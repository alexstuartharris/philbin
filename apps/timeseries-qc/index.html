Time Series QC

Upload or paste a CSV with a timestamp column and a value column to plot the series and get quick QC metrics (cadence inference, duplicates, missing %, gap list, and robust outlier flags). Runs entirely in your browser.

Tip: if your timestamps don\'t parse cleanly, try ISO-8601 (e.g. 2026-02-04T22:30:00Z).

Notes / limitations
  • Timestamp parsing uses Date.parse best-effort; ambiguous formats may misparse.
  • Cadence is computed as the mode of positive time deltas (rounded to nearest second).
  • Outliers are flagged with robust z-score (median/MAD), threshold |z| > 5.
